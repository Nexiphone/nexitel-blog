## Understanding What Happens During Number Porting

When you decide to switch carriers and bring your existing phone number to Nexitel, understanding the porting process helps you avoid unexpected charges and service interruptions. The moment your number successfully ports, specific changes take effect with both your old carrier and your new Nexitel service.

Number portability is a consumer right in the United States, but the mechanics of what happens behind the scenes often confuse people making the switch—especially international students arriving on F-1 visas, H1B workers relocating for new positions, or anyone moving from expensive postpaid contracts to affordable prepaid plans.

## Your Old Account Automatically Cancels

### Immediate Termination Upon Port Completion

The most important thing to understand: **your old carrier account closes automatically the moment your number successfully ports to Nexitel**. You don't need to call your previous carrier to cancel. In fact, calling to cancel before porting will release your number and make porting impossible.

Here's the exact sequence:

1. You submit a port request to Nexitel with your account details
2. Nexitel contacts your old carrier to request the number transfer
3. Your old carrier verifies the information and approves the port
4. The port completes (usually within 2-24 hours for wireless numbers)
5. Your old account automatically terminates at the exact moment the port finishes
6. Your Nexitel service becomes active immediately

### What This Means for Different Account Types

**If you're on a postpaid plan** (monthly billing contract):
- Your account closes mid-cycle in most cases
- You'll receive a final bill for services used up to the port date
- You're responsible for any device payment plans or financing
- Early termination fees may apply if you're under contract

**If you're on a prepaid plan** (like many international students use):
- Your remaining balance is forfeited in most cases
- Carriers rarely refund unused prepaid funds
- Any active add-ons or international calling credits expire
- Auto-pay stops immediately

This automatic cancellation protects your number from being active on two networks simultaneously, which would create technical conflicts.

## Billing After Your Number Ports

### Final Bills from Your Previous Carrier

Expect to receive one final bill from your old carrier, typically within 2-4 weeks after porting. This bill covers:

- **Prorated charges** for the portion of your billing cycle you used
- **Outstanding device payments** if you financed a phone
- **Early termination fees** if applicable (check your contract)
- **Activation or service fees** that weren't yet billed
- **Taxes and regulatory fees** through your last day of service

Most carriers prorate charges to the day your number ports. If your billing cycle runs from the 1st to the 30th and you port on the 15th, you typically pay for half the month.

### What Happens to Prepaid Balances

Unfortunately, **most carriers do not refund unused prepaid balances** when you port your number. This includes:

- Remaining days on your monthly plan
- Unused data, minutes, or text allowances
- International calling credits
- Add-on packages you purchased

**Strategic timing tip**: If you're on a prepaid plan, time your port to occur near the end of your billing cycle to minimize lost value. If your plan renews on the 5th of each month, port on the 3rd or 4th rather than right after renewal.

For H1B workers moving from employer-provided plans or international students switching from tourist SIMs, understanding this timing can save you money—especially when transitioning to Nexitel's affordable [Blue plans starting at $10/month or Purple plans from $5/month](https://www.nexitel.us/plans).

## Your Service Transition

### No Downtime During the Port

When executed correctly, porting creates a seamless transition:

- **Final hours on old carrier**: Your service continues normally until the port completes
- **Port completion moment**: A brief interruption (30 seconds to 2 minutes) while networks switch
- **Immediate Nexitel activation**: Your number becomes active on your new Nexitel Blue (AT&T network) or Purple (T-Mobile network) plan

During the brief switchover moment, you might notice:
- Calls may drop if you're on an active call
- Data connectivity pauses momentarily
- Text messages in transit might deliver slightly delayed

### What to Do When Your Port Completes

You'll know your port finished when:

1. Your old SIM shows "No Service" or "SOS Only"
2. Your Nexitel SIM shows signal bars and carrier name
3. You receive a confirmation text from Nexitel
4. You can make outgoing calls successfully

**Immediate steps after porting**:

- Remove your old carrier's SIM card and insert your Nexitel SIM
- Restart your phone to ensure proper network registration
- Test making a call, sending a text, and using data
- Update your carrier settings if prompted
- Configure your APN settings if data doesn't work automatically

For iPhone users, carrier settings usually update automatically. Android users might need to manually select the network operator or configure APN settings for Nexitel Blue (AT&T network) or Purple (T-Mobile network).

## Device Financing and Equipment

### Outstanding Phone Payments

**Critical point**: Porting your number does NOT cancel your device payment obligations. If you're financing a phone through your old carrier:

- **Payment responsibility continues** until the device is paid off
- **Monthly installments** still appear on your bills
- **Full balance** might be due immediately if stated in your contract
- **Device locks** remain until you satisfy payment terms

Before porting, verify:
- Your remaining device balance
- Whether porting triggers full payment requirements
- Device unlock policies (usually requires full payment)
- Whether your device is compatible with Nexitel's networks

Many international students on F-1 visas and H1B workers finance phones through carriers like AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon. These obligations survive the port and must be resolved separately.

### Unlocking Your Device

To use your phone with Nexitel:

1. **Check if your device is locked**: Insert a different carrier's SIM and see if it works
2. **Request an unlock** from your old carrier if needed (usually free if device is paid off)
3. **Wait for unlock confirmation** (typically 24-48 hours)
4. **Verify compatibility** with Nexitel Blue (AT&T network) or Purple (T-Mobile network)

Most carriers unlock devices for free once you've completed payments and fulfilled contract terms. Some unlock devices for military deployment or international travel even if under contract.

## Common Porting Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Leaving a Contract Early

**Situation**: You're 8 months into a 24-month contract but found better value with Nexitel.

**What happens**:
- Your contract terminates when the port completes
- Early termination fee (ETF) appears on your final bill
- ETFs typically range from $100-$350 depending on remaining months
- Device payments continue or full balance becomes due
- You still save money long-term if Nexitel's plans cost significantly less

**Math example**: A $200 ETF plus $400 remaining device balance ($600 total) might be worth paying if you save $40/month on Nexitel versus your old plan—you break even in 15 months.

### Scenario 2: Moving from Tourist/Temporary SIM

**Situation**: You arrived on an F-1 visa with a tourist SIM or temporary plan, now want permanent service.

**What happens**:
- Tourist SIMs are usually prepaid, so no ETF
- Remaining balance forfeits (can't be transferred or refunded)
- Service stops immediately when port completes
- Any add-ons or international credits expire

**Best practice**: Use your remaining credits before porting. Make international calls, use your data allowance, then port just before renewal.

### Scenario 3: Porting from Family Plan

**Situation**: You're one line on a family plan, want your own Nexitel account.

**What happens**:
- Only your specific line ports and terminates
- Other family members' lines remain active
- The account holder might face:
  - Loss of multi-line discounts
  - Higher per-line costs for remaining lines
  - Potential plan restructuring
- You might owe the account holder for your portion of family plan costs

**Courtesy tip**: Discuss with your family plan administrator before porting, especially if your departure affects their pricing or you share device payments.

### Scenario 4: H1B Worker Leaving Employer Plan

**Situation**: Your employer provided your phone plan, but you're changing jobs or want personal control.

**What happens**:
- If the number is in your name, you can port normally
- If the number belongs to your employer, you cannot port it (need their authorization)
- Employer-owned accounts typically terminate when you leave the company
- You'd need to request a port authorization from company IT/admin

**Alternative**: Many H1B workers prefer getting their own number immediately upon arrival, using [affordable plans like Nexitel Blue from $10/month](https://www.nexitel.us/plans) to maintain consistent contact information through job changes.

## Protecting Yourself During the Port

### Verify Information Accuracy

Before submitting your port request to Nexitel, confirm:

- **Account number** from your old carrier (check your bill or online account)
- **PIN/password** if your carrier requires one for ports
- **Name on account** matches exactly (middle initials, spelling)
- **Billing address** matches your old carrier's records
- **Phone number** is typed correctly

Even small errors (wrong ZIP code, misspelled name) can delay or reject your port, extending your time on expensive service.

### Timing Your Port Strategically

**Best times to port**:
- **End of billing cycle** for postpaid plans (minimize prorated charges)
- **Just before renewal** for prepaid plans (avoid losing full month's payment)
- **Weekday mornings** when carrier support is fully staffed for any issues
- **When you have time** to test service and troubleshoot if needed

**Avoid porting**:
- **Right before important calls** (job interviews, visa appointments)
- **During international travel**
- **When your old carrier's support is closed** (weekends, holidays)
- **Same day you need the number for 2FA codes** or verification

### Document Everything

Keep records of:

- Screenshot of your final account status before porting
- Port confirmation from Nexitel
- Final bill from old carrier when it arrives
- Payment confirmations for any outstanding balances
- Unlock request confirmations if applicable

These documents help resolve any disputes about charges, service dates, or device payments.
