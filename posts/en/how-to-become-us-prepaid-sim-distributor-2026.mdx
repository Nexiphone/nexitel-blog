## Starting Your Journey as a Nexitel Distributor

The prepaid wireless market in the United States continues to grow rapidly, especially among international communities. Indian H1B workers, Chinese students on F-1 visas, Taiwanese families, UAE nationals visiting the US, and Egyptian diaspora members all need affordable, flexible connectivity. As a Nexitel distributor, you can build a profitable business serving these communities while helping newcomers get connected the moment they arrive.

Whether you're running an ethnic grocery store, managing an international student services center, operating a money transfer business, or simply looking to start a side hustle, becoming a Nexitel distributor offers a low-barrier entry into the telecom resale business.

## Why Distribute Nexitel?

Nexitel offers two distinct network options that appeal to different customer segments:

### Nexitel Blue Plans (AT&T Network)
Starting at just $10/month, [Nexitel Blue](https://www.nexitel.us/plans) runs on the AT&T network, providing nationwide coverage that's particularly strong in urban centers where international communities cluster. This is perfect for H1B workers in tech hubs like San Francisco, Seattle, or Austin.

### Nexitel Purple Plans (T-Mobile Network)
From $6/month, [Nexitel Purple](https://www.nexitel.us/plans) operates on T-Mobile's network, offering exceptional value for budget-conscious customers like international students, recent immigrants, or families keeping multiple lines active for children.

### Additional Revenue Streams
- **NexiTalk VoIP**: International calling service starting at $4.99/month, perfect for customers calling India, China, Taiwan, UAE, or Egypt
- **Nexi Volt**: Global mobile recharge service for customers who want to top up their Jio, China Mobile, Chunghwa Telecom, Etisalat, or Vodafone Egypt accounts while in the US

## Distributor Requirements and Qualifications

### Basic Requirements
- **Business Registration**: LLC, sole proprietorship, or corporation registered in the United States
- **Tax ID**: Valid EIN (Employer Identification Number) or SSN for sole proprietors
- **Business Location**: Physical storefront, kiosk, or verified business address (home-based businesses accepted)
- **Initial Investment**: Minimum $500-$1,000 for starter inventory
- **Banking**: US business bank account for receiving payments

### Ideal Distributor Profiles
1. **Ethnic Retail Stores**: Indian grocery stores, Chinese supermarkets, Middle Eastern markets, Taiwanese bakeries
2. **Money Transfer Businesses**: Locations already serving immigrant communities with remittance services
3. **International Student Services**: Campus-adjacent businesses, student housing complexes, university bookstores
4. **Travel Agencies**: Businesses serving tourists and visa holders from India, China, Taiwan, UAE, Egypt
5. **Mobile Repair Shops**: Existing cellular accessory and repair businesses
6. **Community Centers**: Cultural organizations, religious institutions, immigrant support services

## Step-by-Step Process to Become a Distributor

### Step 1: Application Submission
1. Visit the Nexitel distributor portal (contact support for access)
2. Complete the distributor application form
3. Upload required documents:
   - Business registration certificate
   - Tax ID verification
   - Government-issued ID for business owner
   - Proof of business location (lease agreement, utility bill, or property deed)
4. Submit application for review (typically approved within 3-5 business days)

### Step 2: Training and Onboarding
Once approved, you'll receive:
- Access to the distributor portal for order placement and inventory management
- Training materials covering activation procedures, troubleshooting, and customer support
- Marketing materials in multiple languages (English, Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Arabic)
- Point-of-sale materials including posters, shelf talkers, and plan comparison charts

### Step 3: Initial Inventory Order
**Minimum Order Requirements:**
- 50 SIM cards minimum for first order
- Recommended mix: 30 Nexitel Blue + 20 Nexitel Purple
- Wholesale pricing: $2-4 per SIM depending on volume
- Suggested retail price: $10-15 per SIM (includes first month of service on basic plan)

**Payment Terms:**
- First order: Payment in advance via ACH, wire transfer, or business credit card
- Established distributors (after 90 days): Net-30 payment terms available

### Step 4: Activation System Setup
You'll receive credentials for:
- **Distributor Portal**: Web-based activation system accessible from any device
- **API Access** (optional): For high-volume distributors who want to integrate activation into existing POS systems
- **Mobile App**: iOS and Android apps for on-the-go activations and customer support

### Step 5: Launch and Marketing
Nexitel provides:
- Customizable flyers in English, Spanish, Hindi, Mandarin, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic
- Social media graphics sized for Facebook, Instagram, WeChat, and WhatsApp
- Window clings and storefront signage
- Co-op marketing funds for established distributors (after 6 months, minimum 100 activations/month)

## Profit Margins and Revenue Potential

### SIM Card Sales
- **Wholesale cost**: $2-4 per SIM
- **Suggested retail**: $10-15 per SIM
- **Your margin**: $6-13 per activation

### Monthly Residual Commissions
Earn ongoing revenue from active customers:
- **Tier 1** (1-50 active lines): 5% of monthly plan revenue
- **Tier 2** (51-200 active lines): 7.5% of monthly plan revenue
- **Tier 3** (201+ active lines): 10% of monthly plan revenue

**Example**: A distributor with 200 active customers averaging $20/month in plan revenue earns approximately $300/month in residuals.

### Add-on Services
- **NexiTalk activations**: $5-10 commission per signup at [NexiTalk](https://www.nexitel.us/nexitalk)
- **Nexi Volt transactions**: Small commission on international mobile top-ups (Jio, Airtel, China Mobile, etc.)
- **Plan upgrades**: Additional commission when customers upgrade to higher-tier plans

## Activation Process: What Your Customers Experience

### Standard Activation (10-15 minutes)
1. Customer selects plan from [available options](https://www.nexitel.us/plans)
2. You verify customer's ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
3. Enter customer information in distributor portal
4. Assign SIM card to customer account
5. Insert SIM in customer's unlocked phone
6. System sends activation confirmation via SMS
7. Customer makes test call to verify service

### Bulk Activation (for groups)
Some distributors serve international student groups or corporate clients:
- Upload CSV file with customer information
- System processes batch activations
- SIM cards pre-assigned to accounts
- Distribution event coordination (university housing, corporate onboarding)

## Common Questions and Distributor Support

### What phones work with Nexitel?
Both Nexitel Blue and Purple require unlocked GSM phones. Most international phones from India (Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi), China (Huawei, Oppo, Vivo), and UAE work perfectly. iPhones purchased internationally work great.

### How do customers port numbers?
Customers can port existing US numbers from other carriers. You initiate the port request through the distributor portal. Process typically takes 1-3 business days.

### What if a customer has problems?
First-line support is your responsibility, but Nexitel provides:
- 24/7 distributor support line
- Troubleshooting guides in portal
- Network status page
- Escalation path for technical issues

### Can I operate multiple locations?
Yes! Many successful distributors start with one location and expand. Each location can have separate inventory and staff access in the portal.

### What about online sales?
Distributors can sell online with approval. Must ship only within the US and maintain activation quality standards.
