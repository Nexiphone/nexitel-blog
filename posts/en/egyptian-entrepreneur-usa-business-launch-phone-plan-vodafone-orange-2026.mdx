### Time Zone Management for International Calls

Egypt is typically 7-8 hours ahead of US East Coast, 10-11 hours ahead of West Coast (depending on daylight saving time variations).

**Optimal calling windows:**
- **Egyptian morning (8am-11am Egypt time):** US East Coast evening (1pm-4pm), West Coast late morning (10am-1pm)—both sides in business hours
- **Egyptian evening (6pm-9pm Egypt time):** US East Coast late morning (10am-1pm), West Coast early morning (7am-10am)—works for urgent matters

Schedule regular supplier calls during these windows. With [NexiTalk's](https://www.nexitel.us/nexitalk) low rates, even hour-long calls cost just a few dollars instead of $60-120 with traditional carriers.
