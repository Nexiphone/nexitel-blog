## Common Questions from Green Card Holder Couples

### "Should we keep our H1B-era T-Mobile/Verizon plans?"

No! You were paying for postpaid plans because you needed "proof of residency" or your employer required it. Now you're permanent residents. Switch to prepaid and save $100-150/month for both lines. Use that money for your first furniture or car down payment.

### "Can we use these SIMs before getting our Social Security Numbers?"

Yes! Nexitel prepaid plans don't require SSN. Perfect for your first 2-3 weeks when you're waiting for SSN cards. You can use the same numbers forever—no need to change once you get SSN.

### "What about our parents visiting us next year?"

Perfect! Buy them temporary Nexitel Purple plans ($6-10/month) when they visit. They'll have USA numbers for 2-3 months, then you can cancel. Much cheaper than tourist SIM cards at the airport ($40-50).

### "Will our unlocked phones from India work?"

Most modern phones (iPhone 11+, Samsung Galaxy S10+, OnePlus, etc.) work fine on AT&T and T-Mobile. Check your phone's compatibility at https://www.nexitel.us/plans. Worst case, buy unlocked phones on Amazon ($150-300 range).

### "How do we receive OTPs from Indian banks?"

Keep your Jio/Airtel SIM in an old phone connected to WiFi (no roaming needed). When you need OTP, quickly turn on the old phone, receive SMS, turn it off. Or switch to email OTPs where possible (SBI, ICICI, HDFC all offer this now).
