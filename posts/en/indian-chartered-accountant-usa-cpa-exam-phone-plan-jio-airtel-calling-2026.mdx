## Why Traditional US Carriers Don't Work for Indian CPA Candidates

Most Indian professionals arriving in the USA make the mistake of walking into AT&T or Verizon stores and signing up for $60-80/month postpaid plans. Here's why that's a terrible idea when you're focused on CPA prep:

### The Cost Problem

- **AT&T/Verizon postpaid**: $65-85/month with taxes and fees
- **Credit check requirements**: No US credit history means security deposits of $200-400
- **Contract commitments**: 12-24 month obligations when your H1B situation might change
- **International calling**: Extra $15/month for India calling add-ons with limited minutes

When you're spending $2,000-3,500 on CPA review courses (Becker, Wiley, Surgent), $1,200 on exam fees, and potentially $15,000-25,000 on living expenses in cities like New York, Chicago, or San Francisco, every dollar matters.

### The Flexibility Problem

Your situation as an Indian CA in the USA is unique:

- You might work remotely some days to maximize study time
- You could relocate between exam sections (taking FAR in Illinois, then REG in Texas)
- Your H1B employer might transfer you between offices
- You might return to India for 2-3 weeks if a family emergency arises

Postpaid contracts don't accommodate this flexibility. Prepaid plans do.
