## Why Egyptian Graduate Students Need a USA Number Immediately

Your F-1 visa journey is just beginning when you land. Here's what happens in your first 72 hours:

### Day One Essentials
- **University check-in**: Your international student office needs to reach you immediately
- **Housing coordination**: Roommates, landlords, and residential advisors need a USA contact number
- **Campus safety**: Emergency alerts and security notifications require a local number
- **Department meetings**: Your advisor and program coordinator will schedule orientation sessions

### First Week Requirements
- **Bank account**: Chase, Bank of America, and other banks require a USA phone number for account verification and fraud alerts
- **SSN application**: Social Security office communications need a reachable number
- **Health insurance**: University health services and insurance providers need to confirm appointments
- **TA/RA positions**: If you're teaching or researching, the department needs immediate contact

### The Vodafone Egypt Roaming Trap

Many Egyptian students arrive thinking "I'll just use Vodafone roaming for a few days." Here's why that's expensive:

- **Vodafone Egypt USA roaming**: Approximately 20-35 EGP per minute for calls
- **Data roaming**: 50-100 EGP per MB (yes, per megabyte!)
- **Orange Egypt roaming**: Similar rates, equally expensive
- **WE roaming**: Often doesn't work reliably on USA networks

**Real cost example**: One week of orientation with roaming could cost 3,000-5,000 EGP—more than a semester of proper USA service.
