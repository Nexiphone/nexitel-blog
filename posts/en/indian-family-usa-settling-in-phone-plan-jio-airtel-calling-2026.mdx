## Setting Up Your Family's Phone Service After Moving to USA from India

Moving your entire family from India to the United States is exciting but overwhelming. Between immigration paperwork, finding housing, and enrolling kids in school, figuring out phone plans often gets pushed to the last minute. Then you arrive at JFK or SFO, need to call your employer or landlord, and realize your Jio or Airtel SIM won't work here.

This guide covers everything Indian families need: affordable US prepaid plans for everyone, keeping your Indian numbers active for banking and family calls, and cheap ways to call relatives back home.

## Why Indian Families Need Different US Phone Plans Than Students

If you're moving on an H1B, L1, or green card with your spouse and children, your needs differ from international students:

- **Multiple lines needed** — You need service for yourself, spouse, and teenage kids
- **Long-term stability** — Unlike students on F-1 visas who might return after graduation, you're settling permanently
- **Indian banking and OTP access** — You need to keep your ICICI, SBI, or HDFC accounts active with your Indian mobile number
- **Regular calls to India** — Daily conversations with parents, siblings, and extended family in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, or your hometown
- **Budget consciousness** — You're managing rent, groceries, kids' expenses, and possibly supporting family in India

Traditional US carriers like AT&T or Verizon charge $70-90 per line. For a family of four, that's $280-360 monthly — ₹23,000-30,000 per month! That's money better spent on your children's education or sending home.
