## Your Complete Mobile Solution for Studying in America

Congratulations on your acceptance to study in the USA! Whether you're heading to UCLA, NYU, University of Michigan, or any other American university for a semester or full academic year, one of your first challenges will be setting up reliable phone service that doesn't drain your budget.

As a Taiwanese exchange student, you need three things: affordable data and calling in the USA, a way to stay in touch with family back home on 中華電信 (Chunghwa Telecom), Taiwan Mobile, or FarEasTone, and a solution that doesn't lock you into expensive annual contracts.

This guide shows you exactly how to set up your phone for your semester abroad without overpaying.

## Why Your Chunghwa Telecom SIM Won't Work Well in America

Many Taiwanese students arrive in the USA thinking they'll just use international roaming on their 中華電信 or Taiwan Mobile plan. This creates several problems:

- **Expensive roaming fees**: NT$600-800 per day for data roaming adds up to NT$18,000-24,000 per month
- **Poor coverage**: Taiwan carrier roaming partners don't always provide the best network access
- **Complicated billing**: Unexpected charges appear months later on your parents' Taiwan mobile bill
- **No local number**: Your American classmates and professors can't easily reach you

You need a proper USA phone plan, not an expensive roaming solution.

### What About Your Taiwan Number?

Most Taiwanese exchange students handle their home number one of three ways:

**Option 1: Suspend your 中華電信/Taiwan Mobile service**
- Most Taiwan carriers let you suspend service for NT$150-200/month
- Keeps your Taiwan number active
- Reactivate when you return home

**Option 2: Port your Taiwan number to a cheaper plan**
- Switch to a basic NT$199/month plan with minimal usage
- Family can still reach you (you'll forward calls to your USA number)

**Option 3: Cancel and get a new number later**
- If your number isn't important, cancel before leaving
- Get a new Taiwan number when you return

**What if I travel during Thanksgiving or spring break?**

**Will my phone from Taiwan work?**
Most modern phones from 中華電信, Taiwan Mobile, and FarEasTone work in the USA. Make sure:
- Your phone is unlocked (ask your Taiwan carrier before leaving)
- It supports USA LTE bands (most recent iPhones and Samsung phones do)

Absolutely! Many students keep their Nexitel number for years. If you return to Taiwan and come back for graduate school or work, you can reactivate the same number.

**How do I pay my bill from Taiwan if I go home for summer?**
You can pay online with any international credit card. Or pause your service during summer break and reactivate when you return for fall semester.
