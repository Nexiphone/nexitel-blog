## The Medical Student Rotation Reality: Why Regular Tourist Plans Don't Work

Clinical rotations aren't vacations. Your daily reality includes:

- **Hospital communication**: Attendings, residents, and coordinators calling/texting your US number for schedule changes, case discussions, and emergency coverage
- **Irregular hours**: 80+ hour work weeks mean you can't rely on hospital WiFi alone—you need data when you're commuting at 5 AM or grabbing food at midnight
- **Taiwan family coordination**: Your parents, grandparents, and significant other need to reach you despite the 12-15 hour time difference
- **Apartment logistics**: Setting up utilities, coordinating with roommates, handling deliveries during your limited free time
- **Interview coordination**: If you're applying for residency, program directors may call anytime
- **Banking and verification**: Two-factor authentication for your Taiwanese bank accounts (台灣銀行, 國泰世華, 玉山銀行)

Tourist SIM cards with 10-14 day validity won't cut it. Hospital WiFi blocks certain apps. International roaming on Chunghwa Telecom costs NT$399/day or more—that's NT$11,970+ for a 30-day rotation, which is completely unsustainable on a medical student budget.
