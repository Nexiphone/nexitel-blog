Congratulations on taking the bold step to practice nursing in the United States! Whether you're preparing for NCLEX-RN in Taiwan, navigating CGFNS credentialing, or already working at a hospital in California, Texas, or New York, staying connected to family back in Taiwan while managing your new American life is crucial. This guide covers everything Taiwanese nurses need: from understanding US phone requirements to keeping in touch with family using Chunghwa Telecom (中華電信), Taiwan Mobile, or FarEasTone.

## Understanding Your US Phone Needs as a Taiwanese Nurse

### During NCLEX Preparation and Credentialing

Before you even board that EVA Air or China Airlines flight from Taoyuan Airport, you'll need a reliable US phone number for:

- **NCLEX registration confirmation** from Pearson VUE
- **State Board of Nursing communications** (California BRN, Texas BON, etc.)
- **CGFNS credential verification** updates
- **Hospital HR departments** calling for interviews
- **Recruiter contact** for contract nursing positions
- **Two-factor authentication** for US banking apps and healthcare portals
- **Emergency contact** during your transition period

Many Taiwanese nurses make the mistake of trying to use their Taiwan roaming plan from Chunghwa, Taiwan Mobile, or FarEasTone. While convenient initially, international roaming costs NT$300-600+ per day—absolutely unsustainable for a multi-month credentialing process or long-term work.

### What You Actually Need

As a healthcare professional, you need:

- **Unlimited talk and text** for hospital shift coordination
- **High-speed data** for electronic health records (EHR) access, continuing education modules, and telemedicine apps
- **Reliable nationwide coverage** whether you're working in Los Angeles, Houston, or New York
- **Affordable international calling** to update family in Taipei, Taichung, or Kaohsiung
- **No credit check** options (many Taiwanese nurses arrive without US credit history)
