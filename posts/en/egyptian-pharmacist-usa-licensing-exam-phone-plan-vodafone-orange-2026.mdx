## Why Egyptian Pharmacy Graduates Need US Prepaid Service

### The Reality of Pharmacy Licensing in America

The path from Egyptian pharmacy school to US licensure involves multiple steps: credential evaluation through NABP, passing the FPGEE, completing pharmacy practice experience, and finally taking the NAPLEX and MPJE (Multistate Pharmacy Jurisprudence Examination). This process can take 6-18 months or longer.

During this time, you need:

- **A US phone number** for NABP correspondence, testing center confirmations, and program coordinators
- **Reliable data** for accessing examination preparation platforms, checking exam results, and researching residency programs
- **Affordable international calling** to consult with family about financial decisions, discuss visa extensions, or simply stay connected
- **Flexibility** because your timeline may change based on exam dates, visa status, or job opportunities

Traditional US postpaid plans require credit checks (which you won't pass as a new arrival), security deposits ($200-500), annual contracts, and monthly bills of $50-80. That's simply not practical when you're already paying exam fees (FPGEE: $835, NAPLEX: $575, MPJE: $150-200 per state).
