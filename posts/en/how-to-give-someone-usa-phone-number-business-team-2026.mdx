Before you start provisioning lines, clarify what your team actually requires:

## Why This Beats VoIP Apps and Forwarding Services

**VoIP apps** like Google Voice and Skype numbers are fine for personal use, but they have serious limitations for business:

- Calls often show as "Spam Likely" or "Unknown" on caller ID
- SMS delivery is inconsistent, especially for 2FA codes
- Voice quality depends on the app's server load, not a carrier network
- Many banks and services block VoIP numbers from verification

**Call forwarding services** are expensive and clunky:

- You're paying twice: once for the forwarding service, once for the destination carrier
- Lag between rings makes conversations awkward
- Forwarding can fail if either carrier has an outage

## Common Questions from Team Managers

### Can we get consecutive numbers or numbers in the same area code?

You can request a specific area code during activation, subject to availability. Nexitel does not reserve blocks of consecutive numbers, but you can activate multiple lines in the same area code if inventory allows.

### What happens if someone loses their phone?

Log in to your Nexitel account, suspend the line to prevent unauthorized use, then reactivate it on a new device with a new eSIM QR code. The phone number stays the same.

### Can we move a line from Blue to Purple or vice versa?

No. Blue and Purple run on different carrier networks (AT&T vs. T-Mobile), so switching networks means activating a new line with a new number. If you want to change plans within the same network (e.g., BlueDom to BlueConnect), let the current 30-day cycle end, then renew on the new plan.

### How do we handle renewals for a large team?

Go to [nexitel.us/recharge](https://www.nexitel.us/recharge), enter each phone number, and renew the plan. You can process renewals in bulk by opening multiple browser tabs or setting calendar reminders to renew all lines on the same day each month.

### What if we need to shut down lines quickly?

Just stop renewing them. After the 30-day cycle ends, the line deactivates automatically. No cancellation process, no retention calls.
