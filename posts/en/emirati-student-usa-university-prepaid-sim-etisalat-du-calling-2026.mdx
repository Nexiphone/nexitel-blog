## Your Complete Guide to USA Mobile Service as an Emirati Student

Moving from Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or Sharjah to study at an American university is exciting—but staying connected shouldn't drain your scholarship money or family budget. Whether you're heading to NYU, UC Berkeley, MIT, or any US university, you need reliable mobile service that won't cost what Etisalat or du charge back home for international roaming.

This guide covers everything Emirati students need: affordable prepaid plans, calling home to the UAE, and even sending Etisalat or du credit to your family while you're abroad.

## Why Emirati Students Need Prepaid Plans in the USA

**Avoid International Roaming Traps**

If you land at JFK, LAX, or any US airport with your Etisalat or du SIM card still active, international roaming will cost you 30-50 AED per day minimum—that's 900-1,500 AED monthly just to use your UAE number in America. For a student budget, that's unsustainable.

**No Credit History? No Problem**

Most American postpaid carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile retail) require a US credit history and social security number. As a newly arrived F-1 visa student, you don't have either. They'll demand $500-800 security deposits per line.

**Flexibility During Breaks**

Many Emirati students return to the UAE for summer break (June-August) or winter holidays. Prepaid plans let you pause or switch services without cancellation fees or long-term contracts.
