## Understanding Number Porting: Keep Your Number, Switch Your Service

Switching carriers doesn't mean losing your phone number. Number porting lets you move your existing number to a new carrier while maintaining service continuity. Whether you're moving to Nexitel Blue (AT&T network) or Nexitel Purple (T-Mobile network), this guide walks you through every step to ensure a smooth transition with zero downtime.

## Why Port Your Number to Nexitel?

Keeping your existing number means:

- Friends, family, and colleagues can still reach you at the same number
- No need to update contacts across banking apps, work systems, or social media
- Maintain business continuity if you use your number professionally
- Access Nexitel's affordable prepaid plans starting from $5/month

Whether you're on an F-1 visa and need to reduce monthly expenses, an H1B worker looking for better international calling, or simply tired of expensive postpaid contracts, porting to Nexitel keeps your number while cutting your costs.

## Before You Start: Critical Pre-Porting Checklist

### 1. Gather Your Current Account Information

You'll need these details from your current carrier:

- **Account number** (not your phone number — find this on your bill or by calling customer service)
- **Account PIN or password** (some carriers call this a "port-out PIN")
- **Billing name and address** (must match exactly what your current carrier has on file)
- **Phone number** you want to port
- **Last four digits of SSN or Tax ID** associated with the account

**Critical:** Do NOT cancel your current service before starting the port. The port request itself cancels your old service automatically. If you cancel first, your number may be lost forever.

### 2. Check Your Contract Status

- If you're on a postpaid contract, you may owe early termination fees
- Check if you have an outstanding device balance
- Verify there are no unpaid bills (ports can be rejected if your account isn't current)

### 3. Ensure Your Phone Is Compatible

Visit [Nexitel's plans page](https://www.nexitel.us/plans) to verify:

- **Nexitel Blue** runs on AT&T's network — most unlocked GSM phones work
- **Nexitel Purple** runs on T-Mobile's network — excellent for international-friendly phones

Your phone must be:
- **Unlocked** (not locked to another carrier)
- **Compatible** with the network you're choosing

If your phone is locked, contact your current carrier to unlock it. Federal law requires carriers to unlock phones upon request if you've completed your contract obligations.

## Common Porting Problems and Solutions

### Issue: Port Rejected Due to Information Mismatch

**Solution:**
- Double-check your account number with your current carrier
- Verify the name and address match exactly (middle initials, apartment numbers, etc.)
- Confirm your account PIN or password
- Resubmit with corrected information

### Issue: Port Taking Longer Than Expected

**Solution:**
- Postpaid carriers sometimes delay ports due to outstanding balances
- Contact your old carrier to verify there are no billing holds
- Check with Nexitel support for status updates
- Do NOT cancel your old service while waiting

### Issue: Lost Service Before Port Completes

**Solution:**
- If you accidentally canceled your old service, contact that carrier immediately
- Some carriers can restore service temporarily to complete the port
- Act fast — porting windows close quickly after cancellation

### Issue: Phone Shows "No Service" After Port

**Solution:**
- Remove and reinsert your Nexitel SIM card
- Manually select the network in your phone's settings
- Reset network settings (this won't erase your data)
- For Nexitel Blue, select AT&T as your network
- For Nexitel Purple, select T-Mobile as your network

## After Your Port: Optimizing Your New Service

### Update Your Contacts and Accounts

Even though your number stays the same:
- Verify your number displays correctly in your phone settings
- Check that caller ID shows your name properly
- Test two-factor authentication on banking and email accounts

After porting:
1. Dial your voicemail setup number (provided by Nexitel)
2. Create a new voicemail password
3. Record your greeting
4. Test by calling your number from another phone

### Monitor Your First Billing Cycle

- Confirm your old carrier sent a final bill

- Check for any prorated credits from your old service
- Note your Nexitel renewal date

## Tips for a Seamless Port

### Do:
- ✅ Start the port during business hours on a weekday
- ✅ Keep your phone charged and powered on during the process
- ✅ Save important texts and voicemails before porting
- ✅ Have a backup communication method (email, another phone)
- ✅ Port when you're not traveling

### Don't:
- ❌ Cancel your old service before the port completes
- ❌ Change your SIM card before receiving the completion notification
- ❌ Let your old account become past-due
- ❌ Port on a Friday evening (support may be limited over weekends)
- ❌ Attempt to port during a billing dispute with your current carrier
