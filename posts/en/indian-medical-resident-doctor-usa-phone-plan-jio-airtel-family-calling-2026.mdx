## Understanding Your Connectivity Needs as a Medical Resident

### The Unique Challenges Indian Doctors Face

**Hospital Coverage Requirements**: Teaching hospitals have thick concrete walls, basement operating rooms, and underground cafeterias. You need a carrier that penetrates these challenging environments. Missing a page from the attending or an emergency call isn't an option.

**Irregular Income During Training**: PGY-1 residents earn around $60,000-65,000 annually—comfortable but not lavish, especially in expensive cities like Boston or San Francisco. Every rupee (or dollar) counts when you're paying off USMLE exam fees, sending money home, and managing living expenses.

**Family Expectations**: Your parents sacrificed for your MBBS degree and USMLE preparation. They expect regular video calls, updates about your residency, and the ability to reach you in emergencies. Time zone differences mean you're often calling India at odd hours.

**Visa Uncertainty**: J-1 visa holders face return requirements. H-1B holders worry about renewals. You need flexible, no-contract plans that don't lock you into long-term commitments.
