## Why International Roaming from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom Is Extremely Expensive

Many Chinese visitors assume they'll simply activate international roaming on their existing China Mobile (中国移动), China Unicom (中国联通), or China Telecom (中国电信) numbers. While convenient, this approach costs significantly more:

- **Data roaming rates**: Often ¥30-50 RMB per MB or ¥198-298 RMB for limited daily data packages
- **Voice calls**: ¥15-25 RMB per minute for calls back to China, ¥8-15 RMB per minute for local US calls
- **Text messages**: ¥2-5 RMB per SMS
- **Daily accumulation**: A two-week family vacation can easily cost ¥3,000-8,000 RMB just for phone service

For a family of four visiting the US for 14 days, international roaming can exceed ¥15,000-30,000 RMB—more than many flights!
