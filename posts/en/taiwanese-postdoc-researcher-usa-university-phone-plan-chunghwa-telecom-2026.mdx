## Why Traditional US Phone Contracts Don't Work for J-1 Postdocs

Most Taiwanese postdocs arrive in the US with limited credit history and temporary visa status. Major carriers like Verizon, AT&T retail, and T-Mobile stores typically require:

- US Social Security Number (which takes weeks after arrival)
- Credit check and security deposits ($300-500 USD for international researchers)
- Long-term contracts (12-24 months) that don't match your 2-3 year fellowship period
- Cancellation penalties if you need to return to Taiwan for family emergencies

For someone focused on publishing research, securing grants, and building your academic career, these barriers waste valuable time during your critical first weeks in the US.
