## Can You Use Your US Number While Traveling Abroad?

You're heading home for a month, visiting family overseas, or working remotely from another country. Your US bank, employer, and landlord all have your US number. Can you still receive calls and texts without paying roaming fees?

The answer is **Wi-Fi Calling**—a feature that lets your US prepaid number work over any Wi-Fi network, anywhere in the world. No international roaming. No new SIM card needed in the country you're visiting. Just your phone, Wi-Fi, and your existing US number.

This guide explains exactly how Wi-Fi Calling works with prepaid plans, what it can and cannot do, and how to stay reachable on your US number without roaming charges.

## What Is Wi-Fi Calling?

Wi-Fi Calling routes your phone calls and text messages over a Wi-Fi internet connection instead of a cellular tower. When enabled, your phone displays "Wi-Fi Calling" or a similar icon in the status bar.

**Key point:** The person calling you dials your regular US number. They have no idea you're in Mumbai, Shanghai, Cairo, Dubai, or Taipei—the call just connects as if you were in New York or San Francisco.

### What Works Over Wi-Fi Calling

- **Incoming calls** to your US number from any country
- **Outgoing calls** to other US numbers (landlines and mobiles)
- **SMS text messages** sent and received in the US
- **Voicemail** notifications and retrieval

### What Does NOT Work

- **Outgoing calls to local numbers** in the country you're visiting—you can't call a restaurant in Delhi or a friend's local mobile number
- **Local SMS** to non-US numbers
- **Cellular data**—you're on Wi-Fi; there is no roaming data
- **Calls when Wi-Fi is unavailable**—if the connection drops, the call ends

Wi-Fi Calling is a lifeline for staying connected to your US life, not a replacement for a local SIM in the country you're visiting.

## How to Enable Wi-Fi Calling

The steps vary slightly by phone, but the process is similar:

### iPhone (iOS)

1. Go to **Settings** → **Phone** → **Wi-Fi Calling**
2. Toggle **Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone** to ON
3. Enter your emergency address (required for 911 purposes)
4. When connected to Wi-Fi, you'll see "Wi-Fi Calling" in the status bar

### Android

1. Open **Settings** → **Network & Internet** → **Mobile network**
2. Tap **Advanced** → **Wi-Fi Calling**
3. Toggle ON
4. Enter your emergency address
5. The status bar shows "Wi-Fi" or a similar icon when active

**Important:** Some phones call it "Wi-Fi Calling," others say "VoWiFi" or "Internet Calling." Look for any setting related to making calls over Wi-Fi.

## Common Questions

### Can I call local numbers in the country I'm visiting?

**No.** Wi-Fi Calling only reaches US numbers. If you're in Cairo and need to call an Egyptian landline, or in Taiwan and need to reach a Chunghwa Telecom mobile, you need a local SIM or a third-party calling app.

Consider buying a local SIM (Jio/Airtel in India, China Mobile/China Unicom in China, Vodafone Egypt, du/Etisalat in UAE, Taiwan Mobile/Chunghwa) for local calls and mobile data. Use Nexitel's Wi-Fi Calling for US calls and texts.

### What if the Wi-Fi connection is slow or unstable?

Wi-Fi Calling requires a stable broadband connection—at least 1 Mbps up and down. Hotel Wi-Fi, home fiber, and cafe networks usually work fine. Weak or congested public Wi-Fi may cause choppy audio or dropped calls.

**Tip:** If call quality is poor, try moving closer to the router, disconnecting other devices, or switching to a different Wi-Fi network.

### Does Wi-Fi Calling drain my battery?

Slightly more than a regular call, but not dramatically. The phone is using the Wi-Fi radio and VoIP processing. Keep your phone plugged in for long calls, and you'll be fine.

### Can I use Wi-Fi Calling on a tablet or computer?

Nexitel service is mobile-only—you need the SIM or eSIM in a phone. Some phones support "Wi-Fi Calling on Other Devices," letting you answer on an iPad or Mac, but the phone must be nearby and online. Check your phone's settings to enable that feature.

### Does Nexitel support international roaming if I don't have Wi-Fi?

**No.** Nexitel does not offer international roaming on any plan. If you leave the US and have no Wi-Fi, your phone will not connect to foreign cellular towers. Wi-Fi Calling is the only way to use your Nexitel number abroad.

For cellular data and local calls in the country you're visiting, you need a local SIM.
