## Why Indian Healthcare Professionals Need a Smart Phone Strategy During US Credentialing

If you're an Indian nurse or doctor preparing to work in the United States, you already know the credentialing process is intense. Between NCLEX exams, USMLE Steps, visa sponsorship (H-1B or EB-3), and state licensing boards, the last thing you need is communication stress. Yet staying connected with family back in India while managing US hospital requirements demands a reliable, affordable phone plan.

## Why Traditional US Carriers Don't Work for Indian Healthcare Workers

Verizon, AT&T postpaid, and T-Mobile postpaid plans typically require:
- Credit checks (which you won't have as a newcomer)
- SSN verification (takes weeks after arrival)
- $70-90/month base costs
- Additional $10-15/minute for India calls or expensive international plans
- Annual contracts with early termination fees

A nurse earning $65,000-80,000 or a doctor earning $180,000+ can afford these plans eventually—but during credentialing and initial settlement, every dollar matters. You're paying for NCLEX prep courses, visa fees, relocation costs, and sending money home to family.
