## Data Usage Tips for Consultants

**Client site work (weekdays):**
- Rely on client WiFi for large downloads/uploads
- Use mobile data for Slack, email, light browsing
- Tether laptop via hotspot only when necessary

**Hotel/apartment (evenings and weekends):**
- Use hotel WiFi for WhatsApp video calls to India
- Save mobile data for essential use
- Download content (Netflix, Amazon Prime India) over WiFi

**Commute and travel:**
- Stream music reasonably (Spotify, Gaana)
- Avoid HD video streaming on mobile data
- Use offline Google Maps after downloading routes

**Typical monthly usage for consultant:**
- 10-15GB for moderate users (email, Slack, maps, music)
- 20-25GB for heavy users (frequent hotspot, social media, streaming)
- 5-8GB for light users (mostly WiFi reliant)
