## Getting Your US Number Before Takeoff

Landing at JFK, LAX, or O'Hare without a working US phone number creates immediate problems. You can't call your Uber, confirm your hotel, or reach the friend picking you up. But waiting until you land to activate service means standing in an airport hunting for Wi-Fi while jet-lagged.

The solution? Activate your US prepaid eSIM before you leave home. In 2026, most carriers—including Nexitel—let you complete the entire activation process while you're still overseas. Your American number becomes active the moment you step off the plane.

This guide walks through exactly how to get set up before departure, covering device compatibility, activation steps, and what to expect during your first hours in the USA.

## Why Activate Early?

**Immediate connectivity**: The second your plane touches down, your phone connects to AT&T or T-Mobile towers. No hunting for airport Wi-Fi. No trying to remember your account password while people crowd around you.

**Better planning**: Knowing your US number in advance lets you share it with contacts, set up two-factor authentication for US banking apps, and configure delivery notifications before you travel.

**Stress-free arrival**: Immigration officers sometimes ask for a US contact number. Having one active before you land simplifies that conversation.

**Test while you have support**: If something goes wrong during activation, you're still home with your regular internet connection and can troubleshoot easily. Much better than discovering problems at 11 PM in an unfamiliar airport.

## Device Requirements

### eSIM Compatibility

Not every phone supports eSIM. Before purchasing any plan, verify your device is compatible:

- **iPhone**: XS, XS Max, XR, and all models after (2018+)
- **Samsung**: Galaxy S20 and newer flagship models, Z Fold/Flip series
- **Google Pixel**: Pixel 3 and later
- **Other Android**: Many 2022+ models from Motorola, OnePlus, OPPO support eSIM

Check your phone's settings. On iPhone, go to Settings > General > About—if you see an "Available SIM" or "Digital SIM" entry, you're compatible. On Android, look in Settings > Connections > SIM card manager for "Add eSIM" or "Add mobile plan."

### Unlocked Device

Your phone must be carrier-unlocked. If you bought it directly from Jio, China Mobile, Chunghwa Telecom, Etisalat, or Vodafone Egypt on a contract, it might be locked to that carrier. Contact them to request an unlock code before traveling.

Insert a different carrier's physical SIM at home to test—if it works, your phone is unlocked.

## Step-by-Step Activation Process

### Before You Purchase

**1. Confirm your travel dates**: Most prepaid plans activate immediately or within 24 hours. Don't buy too early—purchase 1-3 days before your departure.

**2. Have your passport ready**: You'll need ID information during signup. Keep your passport number handy.

**3. Check your email access**: The eSIM QR code arrives via email. Make sure you can access that email account on a different device (laptop, tablet, or another phone) since you'll need to scan the code with your phone's camera.

**4. Ensure stable internet**: You need reliable Wi-Fi to download the eSIM profile. Hotel Wi-Fi works, but home broadband is better.

### Purchase and Registration

**1. Visit Nexitel's website**: Go to [nexitel.us/plans](https://www.nexitel.us/plans) and select your preferred plan.

**2. Complete checkout**: Enter your email address, create an account, and provide payment information. International credit cards from banks in India (ICICI, HDFC, Axis), China (Bank of China, ICBC), Taiwan (Cathay, Fubon), UAE (Emirates NBD, ADCB), and Egypt (CIB, QNB) are accepted.

**3. Verify your identity**: Upload a photo of your passport or other government ID. This step is required by US regulations for all mobile services.

**4. Choose your number**: You'll be assigned a US number. In some cases, you can select an area code matching your destination city—312 for Chicago, 415 for San Francisco, 713 for Houston, etc.

**5. Receive confirmation**: Within minutes to a few hours, you'll get an email with your account details and eSIM activation QR code.

### Installing the eSIM Profile

This is where your US number gets added to your phone—while you're still at home.

#### iPhone Installation

1. Open the email with your QR code on a computer or different device
2. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Cellular (or Mobile Data)
3. Tap "Add Cellular Plan" or "Add eSIM"
4. Point your camera at the QR code displayed on your other screen
5. Tap "Add Cellular Plan" when prompted
6. Label this plan "Nexitel" or "USA" so you remember which is which
7. Choose whether to use this line for cellular data (select "No" for now—wait until you land)
8. Complete the setup

#### Android Installation

1. Open Settings > Connections > SIM card manager (exact wording varies by manufacturer)
2. Tap "Add mobile plan" or "Add eSIM"
3. Scan the QR code from your email
4. Follow prompts to confirm and label the plan
5. Keep your home SIM as the default for now

### Important: Don't Activate Data Yet

Your eSIM profile is now installed, but don't enable it as your active data line until you're in the USA. If you turn on cellular data while still in India, China, Taiwan, UAE, or Egypt, your phone might try to connect to local towers and fail—or worse, trigger international roaming charges on your home carrier.

Keep your existing home SIM (Jio, Airtel, China Mobile, China Unicom, Chunghwa Telecom, du, Etisalat, Vodafone Egypt, Orange Egypt) as your active line until your plane lands.

## What to Do During Your Flight

**Enable airplane mode**: Like always, turn on airplane mode during the flight.

**Keep Wi-Fi on**: Most international flights to the USA offer Wi-Fi (sometimes paid). You can use this to download apps you'll need—Uber, Lyft, Google Maps—while in the air.

**Prepare your phone settings**: Go into your dual-SIM settings and review which line will be used for cellular data. Don't switch yet, just familiarize yourself with the menu so it's quick when you land.

## Activation Upon Arrival

The moment your plane touches down at the gate:

**1. Disable airplane mode**: Turn off airplane mode in your settings.

**2. Switch your data line**: Go to Settings > Cellular (or Connections) and select your Nexitel eSIM as the active line for cellular data.

**3. Wait 30-60 seconds**: Your phone will search for networks. You'll see "AT&T" appear if you have a Blue plan, or "T-Mobile" if you chose Purple.

**4. Test immediately**: Make a test call to verify voice service. Try 611 (Nexitel customer service, toll-free) or the number of your US contact.

**5. Check data**: Open a web browser or app to confirm data is flowing.

If nothing connects after 2-3 minutes, toggle airplane mode on and off once to force a network refresh. This resolves 90% of connection issues.

## Common Activation Problems

### "No Service" Message

**Solution**: Verify you're in an area with coverage. AT&T and T-Mobile cover all major airports, but some parking structures or underground terminals have weak signals. Walk toward windows or go outside.

### Wrong Network Showing

If you bought a Nexitel Blue plan but your phone shows "T-Mobile," or vice versa:

**Solution**: Go to Settings > Cellular > Network Selection. Turn off "Automatic" and manually select the correct carrier (AT&T for Blue, T-Mobile for Purple). Wait 20 seconds, then turn Automatic back on.

### Data Works But Calls Don't

**Solution**: Some phones need VoLTE (Voice over LTE) enabled for calls to work. Go to Settings > Cellular > your Nexitel line > Voice & Data, and switch to "LTE, VoLTE On."

### eSIM Not Appearing in Settings

**Solution**: Restart your phone completely. If the eSIM still doesn't show, you may need to re-scan the QR code. The code can be scanned multiple times—it doesn't expire.
