## How International Roaming Actually Works

When you travel outside the United States, your phone searches for a compatible cellular network. Your US carrier has agreements with foreign carriers to allow your device to connect. You're now "roaming" on a partner network.

Your carrier must pay the foreign network for every minute, text, and megabyte you use. They pass these costs to you—usually with a significant markup.

### What Most US Prepaid Carriers Charge for Roaming

**Budget prepaid carriers (AT&T Prepaid, Cricket, Metro by T-Mobile):**
- Voice: $1.50–$3.00 per minute
- Text messages: $0.50–$1.00 per message
- Data: $2.00–$5.00 per megabyte (that's $2,000–$5,000 per GB!)

**Premium prepaid carriers:**
- Daily passes: $10–$15 per 24-hour period
- Per-use: Similar to budget carriers
- Some include limited roaming in select countries (often Mexico/Canada only)

**The math:** A typical day in India checking WhatsApp, sending photos, and calling family could easily cost $30–$60 in roaming charges on a traditional prepaid plan.

## Why International Roaming Is So Expensive on Prepaid Plans

1. **Wholesale roaming costs:** Foreign carriers charge your US carrier premium rates
2. **No negotiating power:** Smaller prepaid carriers don't have roaming agreements in many countries
3. **Unexpected data usage:** Apps update, phones sync photos, emails download—all at roaming rates
4. **Lack of transparency:** Many carriers don't warn you before charges accumulate

For Indian students flying home to Delhi, Chinese immigrants visiting family in Guangzhou, or Dubai expats returning to the UAE, these charges become particularly painful for long visits.

## How to Set Up Wi-Fi Calling (5 Minutes)

**On iPhone:**
1. Go to Settings > Cellular > Wi-Fi Calling
2. Toggle "Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone" to ON
3. Confirm your emergency address (required by FCC)
4. Done—your US number now works on Wi-Fi anywhere

**On Android:**
1. Open Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network
2. Enable "Wi-Fi Calling"
3. Update emergency address if prompted
4. Complete—Wi-Fi calling is active
