## Video Call Data Consumption by Platform

Different apps compress video differently. Here's what you can expect per hour of video calling:

### WhatsApp Video Calls
- **Voice-only call:** ~0.5 MB per minute (30 MB per hour)
- **Video call (standard quality):** 5 MB per minute (300 MB per hour)
- **Group video call (3-4 people):** 8-12 MB per minute (480-720 MB per hour)

WhatsApp optimizes heavily for low-bandwidth connections, making it popular for international calls to India, China, Egypt, and Taiwan where cellular networks vary in speed.

### Zoom Meetings
- **Audio-only:** 1-1.5 MB per minute (60-90 MB per hour)
- **Video (720p HD):** 12-15 MB per minute (720-900 MB per hour)
- **Video (1080p HD):** 20-25 MB per minute (1.2-1.5 GB per hour)
- **Group call with gallery view:** 15-30 MB per minute (900 MB-1.8 GB per hour)

Zoom's HD defaults make it data-hungry, but the quality is excellent for professional meetings and university classes.

### FaceTime (Apple Devices)
- **FaceTime Audio:** ~0.6 MB per minute (36 MB per hour)
- **FaceTime Video (standard):** 10-12 MB per minute (600-720 MB per hour)
- **FaceTime Video (HD):** 18-22 MB per minute (1.08-1.32 GB per hour)

FaceTime automatically adjusts quality based on connection speed but can consume significant data when both parties have strong signals.

### WeChat Video Calls
- **Voice call:** ~0.4 MB per minute (24 MB per hour)
- **Video call:** 4-6 MB per minute (240-360 MB per hour)

WeChat's efficiency makes it ideal for calling family in China, especially when cellular data in either country is limited.

### Google Meet
- **Audio-only:** 1 MB per minute (60 MB per hour)
- **Video (360p):** 6-8 MB per minute (360-480 MB per hour)
- **Video (720p HD):** 12-15 MB per minute (720-900 MB per hour)
- **Video (1080p HD):** 20-30 MB per minute (1.2-1.8 GB per hour)

Meet is common in education and business, and like Zoom, it can burn through data quickly on high-quality settings.

## Tips to Reduce Video Call Data Consumption

### Switch to Audio-Only When Possible
Voice calls use 95% less data than video. If you're just catching up and don't need to see each other, turn off the camera.

### Lower Video Quality in App Settings
Most apps let you cap video quality:
- Zoom: Settings → Video → Select 360p or disable HD
- WhatsApp: Settings → Data and Storage Usage → Low data usage
- FaceTime: Automatically adjusts, but works better on Wi-Fi

### Use Wi-Fi Calling for International Numbers
When you're connected to Wi-Fi, enable Wi-Fi Calling on your Nexitel plan to reach US numbers without using cellular data. This is especially useful when traveling abroad or topping up Jio, Airtel, China Mobile, Vodafone Egypt, or Etisalat accounts while maintaining US connectivity.

### Download Content Instead of Streaming During Calls
If you share screens or media during calls, download files beforehand rather than streaming them live.

### Schedule Longer Calls for Wi-Fi Access
Save hour-long family video calls for when you're home or at a café with Wi-Fi, and use cellular data for short check-ins.
