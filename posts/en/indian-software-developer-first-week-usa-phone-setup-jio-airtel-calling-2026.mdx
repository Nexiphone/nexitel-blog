## Why Your Jio/Airtel International Roaming Plan Isn't the Answer

Before you activate that ₹2,999 international roaming pack from Jio or Airtel, understand this: those packs are designed for short tourist trips, not for someone relocating to USA for work.

**The brutal math:**
- Jio's USA roaming: ₹2,999 for 5GB + limited calls (7 days)
- Airtel's USA roaming: ₹3,999 for similar benefits (10 days)
- Vi (Vodafone Idea) USA pack: ₹3,499 (7 days)

If you're staying in USA for even a month, you'd spend ₹12,000-16,000 just on roaming packs. That's $150-200 USD — enough to buy 6+ months of proper USA service.

## What You Actually Need: A Dual-SIM Strategy

Here's the smart approach most successful H1B and L1 workers use:

1. **Keep your Indian number active** (Jio/Airtel/Vi) for banking OTPs, family WhatsApp, important calls
2. **Get a proper USA prepaid SIM** for daily data, local calls, work communication
3. **Use a VoIP service** for cheap India calling that doesn't burn through your plan

Your phone probably supports dual SIM (or eSIM + physical SIM). Use both. Don't choose between India connectivity and USA connectivity — you need both.

## Common Questions from Indian Developers

**Q: Will my unlocked Indian phone work in USA?**

Most modern phones (especially Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, iPhone) bought in India after 2020 support USA LTE bands. If your phone works on Jio 4G, it'll probably work on AT&T/T-Mobile. Check your phone's band compatibility or just try it — Nexitel plans are month-to-month, no risk.

**Q: Can I port my USA number later if I get a job-provided plan?**

Yes! You can port your Nexitel number to AT&T, Verizon, or T-Mobile if your employer provides phone service later. Many H1B workers keep Nexitel as their personal number and get a separate work number.

**Q: What about 5G?**

Nexitel Blue and Purple plans support 5G where available on AT&T and T-Mobile networks. In practice, 4G LTE is fast enough for video calls, navigation, and work apps.

**Q: How do I recharge from India before I arrive?**

You can't pre-activate USA SIM from India (FCC regulations). Order the SIM to your USA address (employer office or temporary accommodation). Or use eSIM activation the moment you land.
