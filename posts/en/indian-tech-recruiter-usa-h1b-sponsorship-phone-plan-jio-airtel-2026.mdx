### Use WiFi Strategically

Most tech companies (especially in recruiting/staffing) have excellent office WiFi. Use it for:

- **Video interviews** (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams)
- **Large file downloads** (resumes, portfolio PDFs)
- **WhatsApp video calls** to family in India during your lunch break (when they're awake in the evening)

Save your cellular data for LinkedIn browsing, email triage, and urgent candidate calls when you're mobile.

As an H1B holder, you have specific needs:

- **You might travel back to India** for visa stamping at the Chennai or Delhi consulate — having a US number that works internationally helps coordinate appointments

- **Green card process ahead** — you'll need a stable US phone number for USCIS notices, attorney calls, biometrics appointments
