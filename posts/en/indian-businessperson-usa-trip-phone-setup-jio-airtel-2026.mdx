## Business Traveler Pro Tips

### Data Management for Professionals

- Download offline maps before leaving hotel WiFi
- Use hotel/office WiFi for large file uploads
- WhatsApp calls use minimal data (1MB per minute)
- Video conferences consume 2-4MB per minute

### Dual-SIM Smart Settings

- Set Nexitel as default for data and US calls
- Keep Jio/Airtel enabled for SMS only (for OTPs)
- Use WhatsApp Business on your Nexitel number
- Forward critical India calls to Google Voice or similar service

### Time Zone Coordination

- EST is 10.5 hours behind IST (9:30 AM IST = 11 PM EST previous day)
- PST is 13.5 hours behind IST (9:30 AM IST = 8 PM PST previous day)
- Schedule India calls during your evening (their morning)
- Use Nexitel data for calendar sync across time zones
